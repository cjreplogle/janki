Janki 3D anatomy — model licence and attributions
=================================================

_jk3d_lowerlimb.glb (and any other _jk3d_*.glb anatomy model here) is a derivative of:

    -"BodyParts3D - The Database Center for Life Science - CC-BY-SA 2.1 Japan"

    -"Z-Anatomy - The open source atlas of anatomy - CC-BY-SA 4.0"

obtained via the GLB export of github.com/nqwrc/3d-anatomy. Only BodyParts3D / Z-Anatomy
meshes are included: none of the non-commercial components of that distribution (the inner
ear, CC BY-NC-SA; the kidney, CC BY-NC), nor its brain / cranial-nerve models.

_jk3d_upperlimb*.glb: the right upper limb and shoulder girdle (bones, muscles, ligaments,
joint capsules, brachial plexus branches, arteries, veins), same sources and treatment.

_jk3d_trunk*.glb: spine, rib cage and sternum, heart chambers and great vessels, lungs and
airway, abdominal and pelvic organs, diaphragm, spinal cord — same sources and treatment. The
kidney is NOT included (it is CC BY-NC in the source distribution). The spinal cord was reshaped
by Janki (narrower thoracic cord with cervical and lumbosacral enlargements; the source cord is the
same width throughout). The spinal arachnoid mater (a layer just inside the dura) and the
dorsal rami of the spinal nerves were modelled by Janki (not in the source).

Changes made by Janki: extracted the right lower limb (bones, muscles, ligaments, joint
capsules, nerves, arteries, veins) plus the lower lumbar spine and pelvis; simplified and
quantized the meshes; flipped mirrored geometry; thickened ligaments (+1 mm) and nerves (to
typical adult calibre); renamed parts by structure. The anterior and posterior cruciate ligaments
were re-modelled by Janki (the source pair was misplaced) from standard attachment sites on
the model's own tibia and femur. The coracoacromial ligament (upper limb) was modelled by Janki between the model's own
coracoid and acromion tips (the source has none). The left limb is shown by mirroring.
