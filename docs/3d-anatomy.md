# 3D anatomy cards

*Lower limb, upper limb, back and brachial plexus — on a 3D model you can turn, zoom and
dissect. Cards for identifying structures, clicking them on the model, origins and
insertions, innervations and key facts, plus an X-ray view and a simulated CT.*

**[⬇ Download the deck (janki-3d-anatomy.apkg, 19 MB)](https://github.com/cjreplogle/janki/releases/download/anatomy3d-deck/janki-3d-anatomy.apkg)**

---

## How to use

**With Janki** (recommended): you don't need this file — install or update Janki and
choose **Tools → Janki: Set up 3D anatomy cards**. Janki adds the same cards, and on any 3D
deck's overview you can switch card types on and off and choose whether the model shows on
the front of recall cards.

**Without Janki** (plain Anki, AnkiMobile, AnkiDroid): import the `.apkg`
(*File → Import*). Only the **ID** cards are active at first; to add another type, open
the Browser, search e.g. `card:Locate deck:"Janki 3D Anatomy"` (Click cards), select all
and *Toggle Suspend*. Card types: `Identify`, `Locate` (Click), `Identify (X-ray)`,
`Attachments`, `Innervation`, `Action` (key facts).

Sync to take the deck — and its 3D models (about 30 MB of media) — to your phone.
